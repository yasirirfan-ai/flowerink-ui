FLOWER INK — FEATURED COLLECTION SECTION

Copy sections/flower-ink-featured-collection.liquid into the theme's sections folder.
Copy assets/flower-ink-featured-collection.css into the theme's assets folder.
Copy assets/flower-ink-collection-navigation.js into the theme's assets folder.
Add "Flower Ink collection" in the theme editor and select the real product collection.

The section uses Shopify product data, images, availability, prices, and variant IDs.
The collection advances every 3 seconds while visible, looping to the beginning.
The arrows still move one product at a time. Mobile supports swiping.
Native Add to bag forms are used for available products with one default variant.
Products needing variant or subscription choices link to their product page.
Sold-out products have a disabled button. Turn cart buttons off for a launch preview.
Optional product metafields: custom.featured_actives and custom.size.

Local client preview: index.html, with read-only product quick views and launch CTAs.
This package has not been installed, rendered, or published on a live Shopify theme.
